form(form) a short project-specific name identifying the physical form of the carrier, for example as a codex, roll, fragment, partial leaf, cutting etc.
Status Optional
Datatype teidata.enumerated
Suggested values include:
codex
leaves in gatherings, bound, read by turning them
roll
membranes sewn or glued end to end, read by unrolling
sheet
a single sheet, kept flat or folded, not part of a codex
faltbuch
a codex whose leaves are unfolded rather than turned
roll-codex
a roll folded and bound as a codex
booklet
a gathering of bifolia with a cover, complete in its text
quire
a complete gathering of bifolia, without a cover
other
another form
unknown
the form isn't known
Note

Definitions for the terms used may typically be provided by a <valList> element in the project schema specification.

Example
<objectDesc form="codex">
 <supportDesc material="mixed">
  <p>Early modern
  <material>parchment</material> and
  <material>paper</material>.</p>
 </supportDesc>
 <layoutDesc>
  <layout ruledLines="25 32"/>
 </layoutDesc>
</objectDesc>

vocabulary.</sch:assert>
