<titleStmt>

<titleStmt> (title statement) groups information about the title of a work and those responsible for its content. [2.2.1. The Title Statement 2.2. The File Description]

Example
<titleStmt>
 <title>Capgrave's Life of St. John Norbert: a machine-readable transcription</title>
 <respStmt>
  <resp>compiled by</resp>
  <name>P.J. Lucas</name>
 </respStmt>
</titleStmt>
SchematronThe title statement opens with the formal title.

<sch:rule context="tei:fileDesc/tei:titleStmt">
<sch:assert id="title-first-formal"
 test="tei:title[1][@type = 'formal']">The title statement opens
with a title typed formal.</sch:assert>
</sch:rule>
SchematronThe title statement has one formal title.

<sch:rule context="tei:fileDesc/tei:titleStmt">
<sch:assert id="title-one-formal"
 test="count(tei:title[@type = 'formal']) = 1">The title statement
has one formal title.</sch:assert>
</sch:rule>
SchematronThe title statement's titles are typed formal, alias or other.

<sch:rule context="tei:fileDesc/tei:titleStmt/tei:title">
<sch:assert id="title-type"
 test="@type = ('formal', 'alias', 'other')">A title in the title statement
is typed formal, alias or other.</sch:assert>
</sch:rule>
SchematronAn alias isn't empty.

<sch:rule context="tei:fileDesc/tei:titleStmt/tei:title">
<sch:assert id="title-alias-words"
 test="not(@type = 'alias') or normalize-space(.) != ''">An alias
isn't empty.</sch:assert>
</sch:rule>
SchematronA title typed other names its source.

<sch:rule context="tei:fileDesc/tei:titleStmt/tei:title">
<sch:assert id="title-other-source"
 role="warning" test="not(@type = 'other') or @source">A title
typed other names its source in @source.</sch:assert>
</sch:rule>
