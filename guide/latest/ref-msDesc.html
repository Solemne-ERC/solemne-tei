<msDesc>

<msDesc> (manuscript description) contains a description of a single identifiable manuscript or other text-bearing object such as an early printed book. [11.1. Overview]

Note

Although the msDesc has primarily been designed with a view to encoding manuscript descriptions, it may also be used for other objects such as early printed books, fascicles, epigraphs, or any text-bearing objects that require substantial description. If an object is not text-bearing or the reasons for describing the object is not primarily the textual content, the more general <object> may be more suitable.

Example
<msDesc>
 <msIdentifier>
  <settlement>Oxford</settlement>
  <repository>Bodleian Library</repository>
  <idno type="Bod">MS Poet. Rawl. D. 169.</idno>
 </msIdentifier>
 <msContents>
  <msItem>
   <author>Geoffrey Chaucer</author>
   <title>The Canterbury Tales</title>
  </msItem>
 </msContents>
 <physDesc>
  <objectDesc>
   <p>A parchment codex of 136 folios, measuring approx
       28 by 19 inches, and containing 24 quires.</p>
   <p>The pages are margined and ruled throughout.</p>
   <p>Four hands have been identified in the manuscript: the first 44
       folios being written in two cursive anglicana scripts, while the
       remainder is for the most part in a mixed secretary hand.</p>
  </objectDesc>
 </physDesc>
</msDesc>
SchematronA manuscript's description has at least one codicological unit; a known manuscript listed in a file's standOff may hold its identifier alone.

<sch:rule context="tei:msDesc">
<sch:let name="register"
 value="normalize-space(/tei:TEI/tei:teiHeader/tei:fileDesc/tei:titleStmt/tei:title[@type = 'formal']/tei:idno[@type = 'type'][1]) = 'register'"/>
<sch:let name="known"
 value="ancestor::tei:standOff and not(starts-with(@xml:id, 'new_manuscript_')) and not($register)"/>
<sch:assert id="manuscript-unit"
 test="tei:msPart or $known">A manuscript's description has at least one
codicological unit (msPart), with its own identifier: the unit holds the physical description, the
date, the hands and the decoration.</sch:assert>
</sch:rule>
SchematronInside a description, only the profile's elements, in their places, and the prose's phrase-level elements.

<sch:rule context="tei:msDesc//*[not(ancestor::*[self::tei:bibl or self::tei:biblStruct or self::tei:listBibl or self::tei:persName or self::tei:placeName or self::tei:orgName][ancestor::tei:msDesc])]">
<sch:let name="profile"
 value="('msIdentifier', 'country', 'region', 'settlement', 'institution', 'repository', 'collection', 'idno', 'altIdentifier', 'msName', 'msPart', 'msContents', 'summary', 'textLang', 'msItem', 'locus', 'locusGrp', 'author', 'title', 'rubric', 'incipit', 'explicit', 'finalRubric', 'colophon', 'note', 'bibl', 'biblStruct', 'listBibl', 'physDesc', 'p', 'objectDesc', 'supportDesc', 'support', 'extent', 'dimensions', 'height', 'width', 'depth', 'foliation', 'collation', 'condition', 'layoutDesc', 'layout', 'handDesc', 'handNote', 'decoDesc', 'decoNote', 'additions', 'bindingDesc', 'binding', 'term', 'history', 'origin', 'origDate', 'origPlace', 'provenance', 'acquisition', 'additional', 'adminInfo', 'recordHist', 'source', 'surrogates', 'persName', 'placeName', 'orgName', 'date', 'foreign', 'hi', 'ref')"/>
<sch:let name="placed"
 value="(not(self::tei:msContents) or parent::tei:msPart) and (not(parent::tei:physDesc/parent::tei:msDesc) or self::tei:bindingDesc) and (not(self::tei:bindingDesc) or ../parent::tei:msDesc) and (not(self::tei:msPart) or parent::tei:msDesc) and (not(parent::tei:adminInfo) or self::tei:recordHist) and (not(self::tei:term) or ancestor::tei:binding)"/>
<sch:assert id="description-elements"
 test="local-name() = $profile and $placed"><<sch:name/>>
isn't one of a manuscript description's elements here. A description holds the profile's elements in
their places: the contents and the physical description on a unit, the binding alone on the
manuscript, no unit inside a unit; its prose holds only locus, names, title, date, foreign, hi and
ref.</sch:assert>
</sch:rule>
