<editorialDecl>

<editorialDecl> (editorial practice declaration) provides details of editorial principles and practices applied during the encoding of a text. [2.3.3. The Editorial Practices Declaration 2.3. The Encoding Description 16.3.2. Declarable Elements]

Example
<editorialDecl>
 <normalization>
  <p>All words converted to Modern American spelling using
     Websters 9th Collegiate dictionary
  </p>
 </normalization>
 <quotation marks="all">
  <p>All opening quotation marks converted to “ all closing
     quotation marks converted to &amp;cdq;.</p>
 </quotation>
</editorialDecl>

 test="every $t in tokenize(normalize-space(@decls), ' ') satisfies (starts-with($t, '#') and exists(/tei:TEI/tei:teiHeader//(tei:correction | tei:segmentation)[@xml:id = substring($t, 2)]))">@decls (<sch:value-of select="normalize-space(@decls)"/>) points to a correction or a segmentation in the

</sch:rule>
