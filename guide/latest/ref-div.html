<div>

n(number) the division's number, as a value: 49 for a heading XLVIIII; a letter after it for a chapter after a numbered one, or for a number repeated
Derived fromatt.global
Status Optional
Datatype token
typecharacterizes the element in some sense, using any convenient classification scheme or typology.
Derived fromatt.typed
Status Required
Datatype teidata.enumerated
Legal values are:
section
a book, a part, or one text inside a document (a council in a collection)
chapter
a numbered or titled unit; chapters may hold chapters
unit
a subdivision without a heading of its own, where several stand side by side: a norm and its authority statements, one signatory among the subscriptions
preface
closer
a closing rubric, or a letter's farewell and date
subscriptions
the signatories
register
the list of chapter titles
registerItem
one entry of a register
part
a part of Gratian's Decretum
distinctio
a distinction of Gratian's Decretum
causa
a causa of Gratian's Decretum
quaestio
a question of a causa
capitulum
a canon of Gratian's Decretum
dictum
Gratian's own words between the canons

Example
<body>
 <div type="part">
  <head>Fallacies of Authority</head>
  <p>The subject of which is Authority in various shapes, and the object, to repress all
     exercise of the reasoning faculty.</p>
  <div n="1" type="chapter">
   <head>The Nature of Authority</head>
   <p>With reference to any proposed measures having for their object the greatest
       happiness of the greatest number [...]</p>
   <div n="1.1" type="section">
    <head>Analysis of Authority</head>
    <p>What on any given occasion is the legitimate weight or influence to be attached to
         authority [...] </p>
   </div>
   <div n="1.2" type="section">
    <head>Appeal to Authority, in What Cases Fallacious.</head>
    <p>Reference to authority is open to the charge of fallacy when [...] </p>
   </div>
  </div>
 </div>
</body>
SchematronUnits are written only where they stand side by side.

<sch:rule context="tei:div[@type = 'unit']">
<sch:assert id="unit-alone"
 test="count(../tei:div) gt 1">A unit stands alone in its division: its
paragraphs belong to the division itself.</sch:assert>
</sch:rule>
SchematronA unit holds paragraphs only.

<sch:rule context="tei:div[@type = 'unit']">
<sch:assert id="unit-holds-text"
 test="not(tei:div) and not(tei:head)">A unit holds paragraphs only,
without headings or divisions.</sch:assert>
</sch:rule>
SchematronA register's items stand in a register.

<sch:rule context="tei:div[@type = 'registerItem']">
<sch:assert id="register-item-in-register"
 test="parent::tei:div[@type = 'register']">A register item
stands in a register.</sch:assert>
</sch:rule>
SchematronA division with a number heading carries the number as a value.

<sch:rule context="tei:div[tei:head[@type = 'number']]">
<sch:assert id="number-heading-n" test="@n">A division with a number heading
(<sch:value-of select="normalize-space(tei:head[@type = 'number'][1])"/>) has @n.</sch:assert>
</sch:rule>
