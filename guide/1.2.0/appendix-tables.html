Appendix C.3 The vocabularies' seeds

The vocabularies that grow, each with the terms it starts with: the union of the Bodleian's lists and the old Solemne guide's; for relation types, the model's and SAWS's; for entry types, biblatex's. A term outside the seeds is reported, not refused. The application's database holds each vocabulary, and the seeds are refreshed from its export at each release; their equivalents in Mirabile, Ligatus and the Getty AAT come with it. Oxford gives the Bodleian's term where the seed is one of them.
