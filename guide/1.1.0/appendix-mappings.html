Appendix E The import formats

Others' TEI comes in through its own schema, then a converter into the guide's forms (VIII.10 The Bodleian's descriptions). For each format, its table says what the conversion does with each of its elements and attributes: keeps it, moves it, makes a record or a relation of it, or drops it, and why.
