Appendix G The sources of the examples

The guide's examples quote short excerpts of the project's corpus, cut where the point is made and revised to the forms this guide decides; marks that don't concern the point may be left out. Each example names its file here, by source: the file's code, the text, and the edition or the manuscript it was transcribed from. An example without a source is made up, where the corpus has nothing to show, and so are the counter-examples.

  1. C.AAC-0816: Council of Aachen, 816, ed. Albert Werminghoff, Concilia aevi Karolini I, MGH Concilia 2.1 (Hannover and Leipzig, 1906), pp. 307–464.
  2. C.AAC-0836: Council of Aachen, 836, ed. Albert Werminghoff, Concilia aevi Karolini II, MGH Concilia 2.2 (Hannover and Leipzig, 1908), pp. 704–767.
  3. C.ASC-0756: Council of Aschheim, 756 or 755–760, ed. Albert Werminghoff, Concilia aevi Karolini I, MGH Concilia 2.1 (Hannover and Leipzig, 1906), pp. 56–58.
  4. C.CAR-0397: Council of Carthage, 397, ed. Charles Munier, Concilia Africae a. 345 – a. 525, CCSL 149 (Turnhout, 1974), pp. 326–341: the file's header and the records of its standOff, the project's own.
  5. C.MAI-0813: Council of Mainz, 813, ed. Albert Werminghoff, Concilia aevi Karolini I, MGH Concilia 2.1 (Hannover and Leipzig, 1906), pp. 258–273.
  6. C.ROM-0826: Council of Rome, 826, ed. Albert Werminghoff, Concilia aevi Karolini II, MGH Concilia 2.2 (Hannover and Leipzig, 1908), pp. 552–583.
  7. C.SOI-0744: Council of Soissons, 744, ed. Albert Werminghoff, Concilia aevi Karolini I, MGH Concilia 2.1 (Hannover and Leipzig, 1906), pp. 33–36.
  8. C.SOI-0744, exported: Council of Soissons, 744, from the edition of Albert Werminghoff, as the template export.xml gives it, as the application would export it; the ids, the records' numbers, the release and the hit of text reuse are made up.
  9. C.VLA-0549: Council of Valencia, 549, ed. José Vives, Concilios visigóticos e hispano-romanos, España Cristiana 1 (Barcelona and Madrid, 1963), pp. 61–64.
  10. C.CON-0381-v5: First Council of Constantinople, 381, ed. Adolf Martin Ritter, in The oecumenical councils: from Nicaea I to Nicaea II (325-787), ed. Giuseppe Alberigo, Corpus Christianorum, Conciliorum Oecumenicorum Generaliumque Decreta 1 (Turnhout, 2006), pp. 35–70: the file's description of its edition.
  11. D.CEL-0428-369: Celestine I, letter to the bishops of Vienne and Narbonne, 428, ed. Pierre Coustant, Epistolae Romanorum pontificum I (Paris, 1721), cols. 1065–1072: the file's description of its edition.
  12. COLL.400.ms-Mun-BSB-4592: Collectio 400 capitulorum, transcribed by the project from Munich, Bayerische Staatsbibliothek, Clm 4592.
  13. COLL.AbboCan.ed-PL139: Abbo of Fleury, Canones, ed. Jacques-Paul Migne, Patrologia Latina, vol. 139 (Paris, 1853), cols. 473A–508A, in Corpus Corporum's text.
  14. COLL.Benedictus: Collection of Benedictus Levita, put together by the project from the edition of Georg Heinrich Pertz, MGH Leges 2.2 (Hannover, 1837), pp. 17–158, and Gerhard Schmitz's of Additio 1 (2008): the file's description of its sources.
  15. COLL.Dach: Collectio Dacheriana, ed. Luc d'Achery, Spicilegium, vol. 11 (Paris, 1723), pp. 1–200.
  16. COLL.DioHad: Collectio Dionysio-Hadriana, ed. Johannes Wendelstinus Cochlaeus, Canones apostolorum. Veterum conciliorum constitutiones. Decreta pontificum antiquiora (Mainz, 1525).
  17. COLL.IsaacCan.ed-PL124: Isaac of Langres, Canones, ed. Jacques-Paul Migne, Patrologia Latina, vol. 124 (Paris, 1852), cols. 1075C–1110C, in Corpus Corporum's text.
  18. COLL.Ans.ms-Par-BnF-15392: Collectio Anselmo dedicata in Paris, Bibliothèque nationale de France, lat. 15392: the file's description of the manuscript.
  19. COLL.Dach.ms-Brn-Burg-425: Collectio Dacheriana in Bern, Burgerbibliothek, 425: the file's description of the manuscript.
  20. COLL.His.ms-Vat-BAV-Pal575: Collectio Hispana in Vatican City, Biblioteca Apostolica Vaticana, Pal. lat. 575: the file's description of the manuscript.
  21. COLL.Laur.ms-Vat-BAV-Pal574: Collectio Laureshamensis in Vatican City, Biblioteca Apostolica Vaticana, Pal. lat. 574: the file's description of the manuscript.
